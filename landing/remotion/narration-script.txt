You close your MacBook lid... and everything stops. Builds die. Downloads freeze. Your AI agents fall asleep, right in the middle of the job.

Not anymore. MacClosedAwake keeps your Mac fully awake — even with the lid closed. No external display. No tricks.

One click from your menu bar, and you're set. Or pick a timer: one, two, four, eight hours... or forever. When time's up, your Mac goes right back to sleeping normally.

brew install mac-closed-awake. Close the lid. Stay awake.
