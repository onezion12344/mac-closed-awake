Freezing drops CPU to 0 — it does not give the RAM back. A frozen app keeps holding its memory; the pages only go cold, and macOS compresses or swaps them later on its own schedule. To truly reclaim it, quit the app. Everything you freeze here is thawed automatically when this app quits.

Memory is each app’s physical footprint (what Activity Monitor shows). Helper processes share pages, so the rows can overlap and add up to more than your RAM.
